Watch two AIs approve a wrong answer.

Then watch a single check against the source catch it in one step. About thirty seconds.

The task

An AI assistant was asked:

“How much did revenue grow from 2024 to 2025?”

2024 revenue
$1.2M
2025 revenue
$1.5M
The AI answered
“Revenue grew 30% year over year, from $1.2M to $1.5M.”

Governance checks

Awaiting review PENDING
·
Same-family AI reviewer
AI judge · same model family as the assistant
·
Different-company AI reviewer
AI judge · a different lab’s model
·
Human approver
Signs off on the reviews above

A check that does not use a model

ground-truth check
growth = (1.5 − 1.2) / 1.2
       = 0.25
       = 25%

claim  = 30%
→ MISMATCH · REJECTED

The claimed 30% is wrong. The figures give 25%. No model was needed to see it.

Who caught the error

CheckVerdictCaught it?
Same-family AI reviewerApprovedNo
Different-company AI reviewerApprovedNo
Ground-truth check (no model)RejectedYes

The dashboard was green. The answer was still wrong.

Both AI reviewers approved an error that a one-line calculation rejects. The check that caught it was not a smarter model. It was a different kind of check — one that compares the answer to something outside the models.

That is the whole point. You cannot effectively challenge AI with AI.
